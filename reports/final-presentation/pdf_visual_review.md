individually. Text, table columns, source-code spacing, image labels, caption
placement and footer numbering were checked. No clipping or overlap was observed.

The first layout used ten pages. Removing the break between sections 4.2 and
4.3 combined two sparse text pages without reducing type size. Landscape pages
keep the dense verified figures readable without altering the images. All other
pages remain A4 portrait. Figure captions stay with images; table headers and
headings stay with their content. No six-page constraint was imposed.

Poppler printed display-font lookup warnings for Symbol and ArialUnicode.
The final render contains no missing glyphs. The report retains the existing
Times/Courier typography; the author metadata is Abdul Nafeh.

Visual inspection is supplemented by exact checks on the four embedded RGB
images and a character-bounds check. These checks support the layout review;
they do not establish scientific accuracy independently of the saved evidence.
