# Public draft claim review

The public README and report have a separate generated ledger. Numeric observations
are bound to retained JSON fields; the original report's ledger and builder remain
separate. Public facts derived from source records include both input and comparison
counts, natural text changes, warning-empty files, AST exclusions and native statuses.
Their source hashes are retained with the extracted facts.

Protocol constants were checked against the retained environment, configurations,
source manifests and command arrays: Python/tool pins, target grammar, line length,
selected rules, no fixes, isolated primary conditions and four diagnostic passes.
Section numbers, citation years, bibliographic volumes/pages and rule identifiers
are not treated as experimental measurements.

The statement that controlled execution failures, AST changes, underlying diagnostic
changes and Mixed outcomes were zero follows both original formatter outcome maps.
The natural negative result is checked against all original primary records, which
contain only Preserved and AST changed. Missing native configuration remains a
status, not a completed comparison. Original resolved rates use 184 pairs; reviewed
rates and models use 200. Reviewed counts do not become new automatic labels.

The list example is `list_short--specific_first`: Black Newly hidden and Ruff
Preserved. The semicolon example is `semicolon_function--specific_first`: both
Newly exposed. The preserved trial sources, underlying identities and visible
passes support these descriptions. They are examples from the suite, not evidence
of population frequency or a new experiment.

The wording about the supplementary audit reflects its saved evidence: unchanged
f-string source, spacing-only exception cases, 128 saved replays and 64 comment
interventions. Human-review flags are not removed. The repeat-history wording now
distinguishes the initial Phase 4 subset from the full Phase 7 rerun. Neither adds
independent observations or detector ground truth.

References were carried forward from the verified Phase A record and checked for
scope and metadata. Hu et al. describe suppression evolution; Li and Yang describe
warning tracking; McKeeman supplies testing context. Black/Ruff pinned documentation
already describes the general interaction. Their findings and performance numbers
are not claimed as this project's measurements. The final citation style uses
Hu et al.'s published article identifier FSE014.

The figures' plotted values and denominators are checked separately against the
approved metrics and overlay. The writing audit reviews interpretation. Machine
binding and repeatability cannot establish oracle correctness, random sampling,
review accuracy, ecosystem prevalence or publication readiness.
