published at [GitHub](https://github.com/AbdulNafeh10/formatter-suppression-study).
Its checks concern that snapshot. The machine-readable manifests retain their
original checkpoint wording and hashes; they are not rewritten for later prose

approved Abdul Nafeh as the copyright-holder wording. [MIT](../LICENSE) covers

The author's resume period is August-November 2026. It is supplied context,
not a reconstruction of development milestones. No historical dates were altered.

## Recorded release checks

Changes are limited to privacy-derived execution metadata and hashes, portable
evidence reading and verification, explicit archive-omission handling, source
containers with modification notices, public packaging documentation and license
inventories. The matcher, classifier, measurement criteria and selected source
bytes were not changed. Original automatic labels and later AI-assisted proposals
remain separate.

All 146 tests passed in a newly installed pinned environment. All 400 controlled
and 80 natural fresh comparisons matched retained outcomes. Fresh downloads of
all three archives matched the original hashes and recreated sample selection.
Offline verification was also tested and explicitly reports its absent archive
checks. Both analyses, all seven PNG/SVG figure pairs and the claim ledgers passed.
The current public PDF has six visually reviewed pages.

The preservation check compared 5,588 measured source/AST/formatter byte sequences,
3,518 result records and 10,922 diagnostic arrays, excluding only execution
filenames from diagnostic equality. It found no scientific mismatches. All 25
checked numeric CSVs, both metrics objects, figure exports and supplementary
judgments were preserved. The historical report and PDF remain byte-identical.

The full upstream archives and their unrelated artwork, certificate/key fixtures
and theme assets are excluded. Required licenses and notices remain; the sampled
Flask-CLI/CERN notice was additionally checked and its full terms retained. Source
containers keep the appropriate attribution with modified source without shifting
measured lines. No permission to redistribute excluded artwork is assumed.

Home/workspace paths and personal contacts were anonymized, and hostnames removed
from JUnit derivatives. Original captures remain private. Local verification
outputs and downloaded archives are outside the distributable tree. Pattern
scanning is not an absolute guarantee about every possible encoded secret.
