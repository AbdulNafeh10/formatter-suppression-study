# AI assistance

AI tools assisted implementation, debugging, controlled template construction,
analysis scripts, literature checks and writing. They also assisted this editorial
revision. Numerical observations came from executed Black and Ruff commands;
source, AST, token, diagnostic and repeat checks used deterministic tools.

The controlled inputs are AI-assisted templates crossed with directive variants.
They are not naturally occurring source observations. The separate repository
sample retains upstream source bytes; its authors' AI use is unknown.

The ambiguity audit is a separate AI-assisted interpretation of saved evidence,
not independent human adjudication. Its 32 proposed Preserved labels are kept
apart from the original automatic classifications. The reviewed analysis depends
on accepting those proposals, and human-interpretation flags remain visible.

The same development process supplied code, examples, tests and explanations.
They can share mistakes. Passing tests, reproducing outputs and checking claim
bindings establish consistency under those checks; they do not establish
independent detector accuracy. No precision, recall or F1 is claimed.

This disclosure makes no claim of sole human authorship. The project author
remains responsible for reviewing the methods, interpretations and attribution
requirements of any submission. Editing for clarity does not establish who
originally wrote a passage, and no AI-detector score was used as a revision target.
